# Double elimination: the WB final winner is champion, the 2nd final decides 2nd and 3rd

Our double elimination follows the user's Polish paper sheet "Tabela rozgrywek 16 zawodników", not the usual international format. In that format, the winners-bracket champion plays the losers-bracket winner in a grand final, sometimes with a reset match.

Here the **WB final** winner is champion and plays no more. The WB final loser plays the losers-bracket winner in the **2nd final**: its winner is 2nd and its loser 3rd. There is no grand final.

We copied the remaining details from the sheet:

- Seed positions are 1, 16, 9, 8, 5, 12, 13, 4, 3, 14, 11, 6, 7, 10, 15, 2.
- Drop-ins are crossed in every losers round, including the E/F drop-ins, where the sheet was ambiguous and we chose to cross.
- Each losers round gives its losers a shared place range (13–16, 9–12, 7–8, 5–6, 4th).

Don't "fix" this to the standard format: it is how these tournaments are actually run.
